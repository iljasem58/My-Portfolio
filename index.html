The RAG platform

Joined Netcracker right as the team was just getting started on this. Since then, I've driven adoption across internal and external users, reprioritized the backlog around real usage, and defended the platform's trade-offs on every client call.

Here's how the platform works, and the trade-off I've spent the most time defending: source systems are chunked and embedded, with the embeddings indexed in OpenSearch and the original text held separately in SeaweedFS object storage. An incoming question from Teams or Webex gets embedded the same way, matched against the index, and the retrieved chunks are pulled back from storage to assemble context before the model answers.

Which model answers depends on who's asking: frontier hosted models for internal R&D and product exploration, self-hosted open-weight models (gpt-oss-120b, Kimi) on owned AWS infrastructure for client-facing traffic, split by data residency, region, and cost. Explaining and defending that split to clients is a routine part of the job.
